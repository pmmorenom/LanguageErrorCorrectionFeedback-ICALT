# Repository for "Analysis and Evaluation of Automated Correction and Feedback for Spanish Language Errors"

This repository contains the code for the correction models presented in the paper "Analysis and Evaluation of Automated Correction and Feedback for Spanish Language Errors", published at the 26th IEEE International Conference on Advanced Learning Technologies (ICALT 2026). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICALT68022.2026.00077


Abstract: This paper evaluates automated grammatical error correction and feedback generation for two underexplored phenomena in Spanish: the impersonal use of tú and the gerund of posteriority. Three models based on different AI paradigms (BiLSTM-LSTM, mT5 and Mistral) are compared in terms of correction performance using the GLEU metric. Results show that only Mistral consistently achieves high correction accuracy across both phenomena. The pedagogical quality of Mistral’s feedback is further analysed through expert evaluation. Findings indicate strong performance in cognitively informative feedback, while metacognitive and motivational aspects are less consistently addressed. Overall, the results highlight the potential of large language models for educational writing support in Spanish.

Presenter: Pedro Manuel Moreno Marcos

Authors: Natalia Centeno Alejandre, Pedro Manuel Moreno-Marcos, Pedro J. Muñoz-Merino, Marina Serrano-Marín and Carlos Delgado Kloos
